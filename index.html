About.

A deeper look at who we are, what we build, and why we chose to do it all for free.

Who We Are

A tiny studio with a very big idea.

Hansse.wg started as a personal experiment: a single repository where one developer decided to gather every tool he ever wanted and never found. No ads. No accounts. No premium tiers.

Today it has grown into a small family of free, browser-based tools — used by students, designers, developers, and everyday people who just need a quick fix without signing up for anything.

Questions

FAQ.

Real answers to real questions — plus a few fun ones.

Are all your tools really free?
+

Yes. Every single tool is 100% free. No subscriptions, no credit cards, no premium versions. Open the page and use it.

Do you store my files or images?
+

No. Everything runs in your own browser. Your files never touch a server — because there is no server to send them to.

Do I need to create an account?
+

Not for most things. You can browse and use all tools without signing in. You only need an account to leave comments or save bookmarks to your profile.

Is the code open source?
+

Yes, everything lives on GitHub at github.com/yassineamine566-byte. You can read, fork, or learn from any part of it.

Do you use cookies or tracking?
+

No tracking cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising. The only storage we use is your own browser's localStorage for preferences (theme, language, accent color).

How do I contact you?
+

You can reach us on Instagram at @only__0.9, by phone at 0669197858, or by opening an issue on GitHub.

Can I use your tools for commercial projects?
+

Absolutely. Everything is provided as-is for both personal and commercial use at no cost.

Are you a robot?
+

No. Hansse.wg is a real project made by a real developer — Yassine. The code was written with help from an AI assistant, but everything here is human-designed and human-maintained.

Will the tools always stay online?
+

Yes. The site is hosted statically on GitHub Pages and requires no servers to run. It is designed to stay online indefinitely.

Is my data safe if I close the tab?
+

Yes. Once you close the tab, everything you were working on is cleared automatically. Nothing is stored on our side at all.

Legal

Privacy.

Short, honest, and human-readable.

The short version

We collect nothing, track nothing, and sell nothing. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

01

What we collect

Almost nothing. We do not ask for your name, email, phone, or payment details. We do not sell or trade any personal information.

02

How our tools handle your files

Every tool processes your files locally in your browser:

  • Your images, text, and files never leave your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server, cloud, or database.
  • Nothing is stored anywhere, so there is nothing to delete later.
  • If you close the tab, everything is gone forever.
03

Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising.

04

Comments & accounts

If you choose to sign in and leave a comment, your name and comment text are stored in your own browser. We do not run a server that stores them.

05

Questions?
