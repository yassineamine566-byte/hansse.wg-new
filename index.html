Our Story

About.

A deeper look at who we are, what we build, and why we chose to do it all for free.

Who We Are

A tiny studio with a very big idea.

Hansse.wg started as a personal experiment: a single repository where one developer decided to gather every tool he ever wanted and never found. No ads. No accounts. No premium tiers. Just clean utilities that do one thing well.

Today it has grown into a small family of free, browser-based tools — used by students, designers, developers, and everyday people who just need a quick fix without signing up for anything.

5+
Free Tools
01
Why We Started

Because the web got messy.

Every online utility seems to demand an account, show five ads before it works, and hide its best features behind a monthly subscription. We wanted to prove that a small, focused tool can just... work. Without any of that.

So we built one. Then another. Then a few more. And we kept them all free — not as a marketing stunt, but as a principle.

02
How We Work

Local-first. Always.

Every tool we build runs entirely inside your browser. Your images, files, and text never leave your device — because there is no server to send them to. No uploads, no cloud, no storage.

This means the tools work offline, load instantly, and protect your privacy by default. It is a better experience for you, and a simpler one for us.

03
What Drives Us

Small tools, sharp focus.

We do not build platforms. We build small, useful things that solve specific problems well. A QR generator. A photo-to-link converter. A handful of utilities that save you thirty seconds a day.

Each tool is a small promise: it will be free, it will be fast, and it will keep working. Nothing more, nothing less.

Our Journey

From one tool to a whole toolkit.

The Beginning
One tool, one problem.

It all started with a small QR generator. Every website online was full of ads, redirects, and forced sign-ups — so we built a cleaner version and hosted it on GitHub Pages.

The Idea
Why not make everything free?

If one tool could be free, why not all of them? We decided to keep every project open-source, advertisement-free, and running entirely in the browser.

Today
A growing, open collection.

We now host multiple tools for image handling, link generation, and daily utilities. Each one is designed to be fast, accessible, and safe.

Tomorrow
More tools. Still free.

Our roadmap is simple: keep shipping small tools that solve real problems. Stay free. Stay open. Stay private by default.

What We Believe

Four rules we never break.

Free, not freemium.

No feature is ever locked behind a paywall. What you see is what you get — completely, immediately, forever.

Your data is yours.

Files you upload stay on your device. We never see them, never store them, and never send them anywhere.

Open by default.

Every repository is public. Read it, fork it, learn from it. Our tools get better because you can see how they work.

Built to last.

No sudden shutdowns. No disappearing features. The tools you rely on today will work tomorrow — and in five years.

05
What We Use

Simple stack, sharp results.

We keep our tech stack small on purpose. No heavy frameworks, no build pipelines, no tracking libraries. Just modern browser APIs and clean, hand-written code that runs as fast as the browser can render it.

Legal

Privacy.

Short, honest, and human-readable.

The short version

We collect nothing, track nothing, and sell nothing. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.

01

What we collect

Almost nothing. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. We do not sell, share, or trade any personal information — because we simply do not have any to sell.

02

How our tools handle your files

Every tool processes your files locally in your browser:

  • Your images, text, and files never leave your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server, cloud, or database.
  • Nothing is stored anywhere, so there is nothing to delete later.
  • If you close the tab, everything is gone forever.
03

Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising. There are no "accept cookies" banners because there are no cookies to accept.

04

Hosting

This website is hosted for free on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may collect standard server logs. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them in any way.

05

Third-party links

Our pages link to GitHub, Instagram, and email. Once you click one of those links, you are on someone else's platform and their privacy rules apply.

06

Security

The site uses a strict Content Security Policy, disables all unnecessary browser permissions, and never executes remote code.

07

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for you to request, correct, or delete from us. To clear stored preferences: Settings → Reset all settings.

08

Questions?

If you have any question, reach out on Instagram at @only__0.9 or send an email. We read every message.
